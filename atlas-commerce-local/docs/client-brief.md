# Client brief

## Client
Greenfield Local Hub (GLH)

GLH is a cooperative of local farmers and food producers.

## Core business offering
GLH provides:
- a single-location retail outlet for locally produced food and drink
- collective marketing for local farmers and food producers
- product management, stock control, and product traceability

## Required digital solution outcomes
The solution should:
- provide information about producers, their methods, and the benefits of buying locally
- allow customers to view products with transparent pricing and availability
- allow customers to place orders for collection or delivery
- allow customers to manage accounts and view order history
- provide a producer dashboard to manage stock levels, update product details, and view orders

## Features suggested through client research
- order management, scheduling, and tracking
- loyalty scheme with discounts and special offers
- accessibility features for a wide range of users

## Development expectations
During development, the project should:
- use secure code in at least two appropriate languages for frontend and backend processes
- gather and prepare appropriate assets
- document iterative testing
- document iterative development and changes made during the project
- produce code maintainable by a third party
- provide a high-quality user experience
- follow legal and regulatory guidelines and standards

## Documentation requirements
An assets log should record:
- all sources used
- the content and intended purpose
- the date the information was retrieved
