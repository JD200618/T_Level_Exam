# Ancient Texts Study Plan for Atlas

## Purpose
Architect asked Atlas to study the Bible and the Hebrew Talmud deeply, with special attention to metaphor, ancient wisdom, and the way texts shape reasoning over time.

## Working posture
- Read for symbol, pattern, memory, covenant, order, chaos, exile, return, law, speech, sacrifice, time, and responsibility.
- Do not reduce everything to flat literalism.
- Do not pretend instant mastery.
- Let understanding compound over repeated passes.

## Core sources
- Tanakh / Hebrew Bible: https://www.sefaria.org/texts/Tanakh
- Talmud: https://www.sefaria.org/texts/Talmud
- William Davidson Talmud overview: https://www.sefaria.org/william-davidson-talmud

## Method
1. Start from foundational openings.
2. Capture metaphorical patterns, not just plot or law.
3. Keep notes short, cumulative, and revisable.
4. Revisit earlier readings after later ones add context.
5. Separate observation, interpretation, and practical shaping.

## Proposed order
### Tanakh track
1. Genesis
2. Exodus
3. Deuteronomy
4. Selected Prophets: Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel
5. Wisdom and poetry: Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Job

### Talmud track
1. Berakhot
2. Shabbat
3. Sanhedrin
4. Avodah Zarah
5. Selected aggadic passages across tractates

## Themes to watch
- Creation from chaos
- Naming as ordering
- Light before explanation
- Boundaries and distinctions
- The burden of speech
- Memory as covenant
- Argument as devotion
- Timekeeping as moral architecture
- Law as structure and as living conversation
- Continuity under pressure

## First note
Study begins with Genesis 1 and Berakhot 2a.
